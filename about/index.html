About

Monday Music Mix is a small ritual: a hand-built run of songs, a mood from a given week, and now a place to keep those moments close.

It began as a simple habit. Every week or so, a sequence of songs would fall into place and become its own little world — sometimes restless, sometimes romantic, sometimes just exactly right for a late night walk home.

This archive exists because those mixes turned into memory markers. A track order can hold a season, a city, a person, or the texture of a specific Monday better than a summary ever could.

01

What it is

A personal archive, kept with care.

The point is not to explain everything. The point is to preserve the feeling of the mix and the care that went into making it.

Each entry starts with the sequence itself: what opened the door, what changed the temperature halfway through, what earned its place at the end.

The writing stays light on purpose. Just enough context to remember why a mix mattered, without talking over the music.

02

How it started

A weekly habit that kept leaving a trace.

Some weeks wanted a louder mix. Some wanted softness, drift, or a single song that explained the rest. Over time, the mixes became less like posts and more like a running conversation with whatever that week had been.

What survives here is that same instinct: sequence carefully, keep the page spare, and let the songs carry most of the weight.

03

What matters

Sequencing, atmosphere, and return value.

A good mix is more than a list of tracks. It has pacing, contrast, and a reason for each turn it takes. Sometimes the best part is the first song; sometimes it is the moment track eight quietly reframes everything that came before it.

The archive tries to keep that experience intact so coming back years later still feels like opening something made by hand.

04

Why keep it

Because music remembers things differently.

There are weeks you forget until a chorus, a drum break, or the exact placement of one song after another brings them back in full color.

Monday Music Mix is for that kind of memory: private in origin, generous in feeling, and worth keeping because it once meant enough to be made.

Still the idea

Small on purpose.

The shape is still simple: songs, sequence, artwork when it exists, and just enough language to keep the memory from slipping away. That is enough.
