About

Monday Music Mix started in December 2010 with a simple plan: a new mix every Monday, made from the most interesting music I had come across that week.

The idea was to introduce people to music they might not find on their own. The mixes leaned indie, with the odd track from somewhere else to keep things interesting. Each one went out as a zip of MP3s, and the early ones came with a Grooveshark playlist for anyone who would rather stream.

Between 2010 and 2013 there were 36 of them. Most went out on a Monday, but not all of them. Mix 17 went out on a Thursday, mix 19 on a Friday, and mix 36 a week and a day late.

01

What's here

Every mix, the way it went out.

Each page has the songs in their original order, the cover art from the original post, and what I wrote at the time.

I fixed a few typos and took out the old download links and the requests for comments, since none of that works any more. Everything else is as it was, including the weeks I was tired, running late, or ready to give up on the whole thing.

Every mix plays here as a YouTube playlist, so you can listen straight through from the first song to the last.

02

How it started

A weekly habit that almost stopped at nine.

By the ninth mix I was convinced nobody was listening, so I wrote that it would probably be the last one. A week of friends pushing back changed my mind, and the tenth went out with a note that said, more or less, fine, you win.

The twelfth mix was built from songs readers suggested. After the sixteenth, in April 2011, the mixes came less often.

03

Why it stopped

It stopped when work got busy.

Mix 36 went out in November 2013, and it was the last one for a long time.

S, my girlfriend back then and my wife now, likes to joke that the mixes stopped because they had already won her over. She might be right.

04

What matters

The order matters as much as the songs.

A good mix is more than a list of songs, and a lot of what makes it work is the order. So every page keeps the original running order, and the record at the top has one groove for each song.

05

Why keep it

The notes say where I was.

Reading back through these, the notes put each mix in its week: the week after my birthday, the month I moved to New Delhi, the week friends talked me out of stopping.

Music is good at holding on to that sort of thing, and that's reason enough to keep them.
